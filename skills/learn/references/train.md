# Practice on something they already do

One practice session and its log line in the state. **They already do this**; someone who
cannot do it at all is learning it, and that is a lesson in the same topic.

## Steps

1. **Isolate one part and say it** before the first repetition: "this series is only about
   the openings of your paragraphs; we are not looking at the rest today". Pick it from
   evidence, the last corrections and log lines, and hold the line: correct nothing
   outside it.
2. **Set the level at the edge:**

   | Repetitions look like | Means | Do |
   |---|---|---|
   | clean and quick | too easy: exercise, not practice | raise it now |
   | costly, with gaps they can name | the edge | stay, change the case |
   | failing, and they cannot say which part | too hard | drop a step, remove one variable |

   Clean twice means the level moved: raise it in the same session.
3. **Run three to six repetitions** on the same part, each on a new case and corrected
   before the next starts.
4. **Ask how sure they are at the first and at the last repetition only.** Sure and wrong in
   the middle still gets its log mention and goes back to 1 day.
5. **Close the series** on one of three endings:
   - **stable** across different cases: one good attempt is luck;
   - **the level moved:** clean twice at the raised level too; the next session picks
     another part;
   - **corrections stop landing:** the same correction three times with no change means
     something underneath is missing; say so and go back to a lesson.
6. **Write the log line:** the part practised, the repetitions, what changed between the
   first and the last, and the ending.

```markdown
- 2026-11-08 · 4 · practice, part: chord changes from G to C at 70 bpm. Five repetitions,
  each a different song. 1–2 stopped at the change; 3–5 clean and in time. Not sure at
  the start, sure at the end, both right. Raised to 80 bpm at 4. Stable.
```

## Done when

The session ended on one of the three endings, said with why, and the log names the part
practised so the next session does not decide it again.
