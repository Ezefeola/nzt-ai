# User manual

Produces `Docs/Manual/<audience>.html` — one interactive file per audience — with images in
`Docs/Manual/assets/`. **On request, at the end of what is delivered.** Once it exists it is a
product document every close keeps current. The only artifact the **end user** reads.

## Steps

1. **Plan it** (`nzt-plan`): the audiences — one file each, never per module —, the chapters
   as the reader's tasks, what gets no chapter and why. Units: the shell (audiences, contents,
   *start here*), one chapter per task, a final coherence pass.
2. **Write from what is on disk**: stories and criteria, test evidence, glossary terms. Capture
   screens from the product as delivered, while writing.
3. **Chapters are goals the reader would say** (*"Cobrar un pedido"*, never *"Módulo de
   cobros"*), in the order the work happens. Each opens with what they will have at the end
   and how long it takes, says what it assumes with a link, and gives **one action per step
   with what they will see**. The front page answers *what do I do first?*
4. **Tone**: second person, present, active, in the reader's language and the glossary's
   words — never a table, endpoint or class. No *just*, *simply*, *obviously*; no cheering or
   emoji. Nothing is the reader's fault: *this happens, here is how you get out*, with the real
   message.
5. **Looks like the product**: the design system's theme, never a second palette; legible
   (65–75 character measure); screenshots from evidence or the mockup, cropped, real people's
   data replaced, re-captured when the screen changes — never an invented screen.
6. **Interactive where it helps**: search, a contents list that follows the reader, long
   procedures collapsing with a checklist (persisted when storage allows, never breaking when
   it is blocked), copy buttons. No build, CDN or network font — it opens from a pen drive.
   Phone and print are first-class. Nothing that pretends to be the product.
7. **Only what was verified**: every step traces to a criterion that passed; unverified gets
   no chapter, planned nothing. No `TODO` on the reader's page.
   A header comment names its stories, screenshot sources and date.
8. **Measure** (`review.md`): width, contrast, the keyboard walk including search and every
   collapsible, and **open it with storage blocked**. Then `generic.md`'s pass on the render.

Propose it with what it is worth and accept a no: an abandoned manual is deleted, not left to
rot.

## Done when

Someone who never saw the product could finish their first real task with only this file, and
not feel stupid once.
