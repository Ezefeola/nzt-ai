# Train — deliberate practice on something they already do

Produces one training session and its rows in `Learn/<topic>/progress.md`. **They already do
this**; someone who cannot produce it at all is learning it — `teach.md`, same topic.

## Steps

1. **Isolate one component and say it** before the first repetition: "this series is only
   about naming; we are not looking at the rest today". Pick it from evidence — the last
   corrections and calibration rows — and hold the line: correct nothing outside it.
2. **Set the level at the edge**:

   | Repetitions look like | Means | Do |
   |---|---|---|
   | clean and quick | too easy — exercising, not training | raise it now |
   | costly, gaps they can name | the edge | stay, change the case |
   | failing, cannot say which part | too hard | drop a step, cut one variable |

   Clean twice means the level moved: raise it in the same session.
3. **Run three to six repetitions**, each a new case on the same component, each corrected
   (`tutor.md`) before the next starts.
4. **Ask confidence at the first and the closing repetition only.** A `high / wrong` mid-series
   still gets its row and the review jump.
5. **Close the series** on one of three endings:
   - **stable** across different cases — one good attempt is luck;
   - **the level moved** — clean twice at the raised level too; the next session picks
     another component;
   - **corrections stop landing** — same correction, third repetition, no change: something
     underneath is missing. Say so and switch to `teach.md`.
6. **Write the log line**: the component, the repetitions, what changed between first and
   last, the ending. The objective stays `in progress` with that evidence.

```markdown
- 2026-09-24 · OA-03 · training series, component: choosing a projection's columns. Five
  repetitions, five cases. 1–2 kept whole entities; 3–5 projected and justified every
  column. Confidence low at start, high at close, both right. Level raised at 4. Stable.
```

## Done when

The session ended on one of the three endings, said with why, and `progress.md` names the
component so the next session does not re-decide it.
