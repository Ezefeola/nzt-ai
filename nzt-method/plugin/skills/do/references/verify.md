# Verifying a unit

Verifying is checking the result against the done criterion, line by line, with evidence.
It is never an opinion ("looks good", "should work").

## How to check

Pick, for each line of the criterion, the check that actually shows it:

| The result is | Check it by |
|---|---|
| Something that runs (code, a formula, a script) | running it on the cases of the criterion, including the one that failed before |
| A document | rereading it as its reader would, against what the reader comes to do; for something important, a review by someone without the conversation's context (a subagent) |
| A list, a budget, a schedule | comparing it item by item with its source and its limits (totals, dates, the budget) |
| Something in the world (a booking, a delivery) | the person's evidence: a confirmation, a photo, a receipt |
| Something the person learned | an exercise they solve alone |

If a line cannot be checked here, it is not checked: say so and say what would check it.

## The verdict

| Verdict | When |
|---|---|
| **done** | every line of the criterion was checked and holds |
| **partial** | some lines hold and others failed or could not be checked |
| **not achieved** | the main line fails, or nothing could be checked |

Never round up: a single unchecked line makes it *partial*. Record what failed and what was
not checked as visibly as what passed.

## When it fails

Try to fix it within the unit if the fix is small and inside what was approved; otherwise
stop with the verdict as it is and propose what to do. Never widen the unit silently.
