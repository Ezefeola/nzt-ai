# Reviews, because forgetting is the default

Runs the reviews that are due. Each review moves its objective on the
ladder of the skill's rules and leaves one *Next review* date per objective in the state.

## Steps

1. **At the start of a session, send the overdue reviews before anything new**, in the same
   message: the questions themselves, with the batch's size ("three quick questions from
   last week, about ten minutes") and that they can skip them and go straight on. Asking
   first whether to review only costs a round trip. A skipped review stays overdue and
   comes first again next time.
2. **Ask them to bring it back from memory; never reopen the lesson.** Reading it again
   together feels good for an hour and changes nothing.
3. **Three or four questions or small tasks**, each on a case they have not practised. On
   anything wrong, ask how sure they were.
4. **Move each objective on the ladder:** a good answer climbs one step; a failed one goes
   back to 1 day, which is the review working. Teach again only after it fails twice.
5. **Write one log line per review**, passed, failed or skipped, and leave exactly one *Next
   review* date per objective.

```markdown
- 2026-11-15 · 2 · review at 1 week, new sheet. Brought it back unaided. Next in 3 weeks.
- 2026-11-15 · 1 · review at 3 days. Could not say what the fourth argument does; was
  sure. Back to 1 day.
```

## Done when

Someone opening the state could tell, per objective, when it is next reviewed and why it
sits on that step.
