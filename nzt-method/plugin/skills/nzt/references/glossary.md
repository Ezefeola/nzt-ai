# The work's glossary

`Docs/General/glossary.md` fixes the key words of one work and what they mean *in this work*, so
the same thing always has the same name, in the conversation and in every document.

## Rules

- **The person's words win.** A package's vocabulary is a starting point; when the person
  calls something differently, theirs is the term.
- **Create it with the first term that needs fixing**, in any size, including direct work:
  a word the person uses in a particular sense, a name for a part of the work, two words
  that could be confused.
- **One term per concept.** Never two words for the same thing, never one word for two.
- **Update it when a term appears or changes.** A changed term is replaced and the change
  is recorded in *Decisions* of `Plan/state.md`, if there is one.
- **Do not invent jargon.** A term goes in because the work uses it, not to look precise.

## Template

Alphabetical order. Written in the person's language; only the heading is fixed.

```markdown
# Glossary

| Term | What it means in this work | Not to confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| … | … | a similar term, if any |
```
