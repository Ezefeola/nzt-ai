# Correcting without doing it for them

A delivery that came back and a person stuck halfway follow the same steps. The correction
happens in the conversation; in a topic it also leaves the objective's status and a log
line in the state.

## Steps

1. **Ask how sure they are before you look:** quite sure or not sure, before saying
   anything about the result, or you measure your own hint. Then:

   | Sure | Result | Means | Do |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | yes | right | acquired | ready for the assessment, if the case was new |
   | **yes** | **wrong** | **they think they know, and will not check again** | correct it; back to 1 day |
   | no | right | not settled yet | another case at the same step, not more explaining |
   | no | wrong | a plain gap | back to the lesson |

2. **Give one hint per request**, each a step further than the last:

   | Hint | You give |
   |---|---|
   | 1 | a reframe, or the question they should be asking |
   | 2 | the missing piece named, not applied |
   | 3 | the next step only |
   | 4 | the solution with its reasoning, recorded as `done with help` |

   Between hints ask what they tried; nothing tried since the last one gets the same hint,
   said differently. Never jump ahead because the exchange is long.
3. **Point at the place and its consequence**: "the loop condition: with an empty list it
   never runs", "the third argument: with `TRUE` it brings the nearest code, not the
   exact one". Never a rewritten version: it would land in their work as something they
   did not do. One or two points per round, the one blocking the objective first. Name
   what is right too, so the next attempt keeps it. Correct against the objective and its
   brief, not your taste.
4. **After the fix, they explain why it works**, in their words, without the material. Your
   own words played back mean it is still open: a new case at the same step.
5. **When they ask for the solution, give it**: no negotiation, no reproach. Record it as
   `done with help`; the next case is a new one at *half done*.

## Done when

They produced the fix and explained it, and, in a topic, the log line says the hints used,
how sure they were and whether they were right.
